Questions we hear often

What silo sizes do you make?

Standard sizes are 60, 100, 150, 200 and 250 ton. Larger silos up to 1000 ton are made on request.

What is the difference between welded and bolted silos?

Welded silos are fabricated and welded. Bolted silos are assembled from bolted panels, which makes them easier to transport and erect. Tell us your site and we will suggest one.

Do you install the equipment?

Yes. We offer erection and commissioning, on site balancing and impeller refurbishment.
